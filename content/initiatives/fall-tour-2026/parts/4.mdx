I put a half marathon and a full marathon on the calendar a week apart. Now
I have to go run them.

Before the San Antonio race, I'll get another chance to see friends from UT
Dallas in Austin. A reunion is a good enough reason to make a weekend of it,
even before the running starts.

## One more reason to cheer

My sister's graduation brings me to Reno. I'll be spending part of this last
stretch celebrating her, with no racing bib required.

## Then there's the marathon

I'll head home after San Antonio, then leave again for Honolulu. The flight
from Reno includes a breakfast stop in Long Beach, which is one way to spend
the day before a marathon.

The Honolulu Marathon will finish the tour on December 13. I'll be home in
Las Vegas the next day, with a break from airports and road races well overdue.
