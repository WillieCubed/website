# WillieCubed brand guidelines

The rules for using Willie Chalmers III’s WillieCubed logo. The page is https://willie.page/brand; the same rules as JSON are at https://willie.page/brand/guidelines.json, and every file is in https://willie.page/brand/williecubed-brand.zip.

## Choosing a version

1. A platform asks for an app icon, favicon, avatar, or link preview: Use the platform files in web/, apple/, android/, and social/. They follow each platform’s template, so leave their artwork as it is.
2. Introducing me to people who might not know the cube: Name lockup, on light (`willie-chalmers-iii-lockup-ink`, light backgrounds) or Name lockup, on dark (`willie-chalmers-iii-lockup-paper`, dark backgrounds).
3. Crediting a project I make as WillieCubed: williecubed lockup, on light (`williecubed-lockup-ink`, light backgrounds) or williecubed lockup, on dark (`williecubed-lockup-paper`, dark backgrounds).
4. Naming WillieCubed where the cube is already nearby: Wordmark, on light (`williecubed-wordmark-ink`, light backgrounds) or Wordmark, on dark (`williecubed-wordmark-paper`, dark backgrounds).
5. Showing the cube on its own: The cube (`williecubed-mark`, any backgrounds).
6. Placing the cube where the tile would clash with the background: Cube on light (`williecubed-cube-on-light`, light backgrounds) or Cube on dark (`williecubed-cube-on-dark`, dark backgrounds).
7. Printing in one ink, embossing, or placing it on a photo: One color, black (`williecubed-cube-black`, light backgrounds) or One color, white (`williecubed-cube-white`, dark backgrounds).
8. Showing a mark at 48px tall or smaller: Use that file’s small version, whose name ends in -small. Its wider gaps keep the faces apart. Lockups and the wordmark have no small version, so keep them at or above their minimum size.

## What to avoid

- Don’t rotate or tilt the logo. The cube’s angles are what make it read as a cube.
- Don’t stretch, squash, or skew it. Scale it evenly, from a corner.
- Don’t recolor the faces or change their order. Use a one-color version when you need a single color.
- Don’t add shadows, glows, outlines, gradients, or bevels. The logo is flat on purpose.
- Don’t put text or other graphics inside the clear space. Leave x clear on every side.
- Don’t use a regular version at 48px or smaller. Its gaps close up. Use its small version instead.
- Don’t redraw the cube, rearrange a lockup, or set my name in another typeface. Use the files as they are.

## Exceptions

- Platform icon files in web/, apple/, android/, and social/ follow each platform’s own template. Clear space and minimum sizes apply to the logo files in mark/ and lockups/.
