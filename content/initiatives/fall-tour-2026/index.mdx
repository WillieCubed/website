Every fall I leave Las Vegas for a few weeks and stuff as much life as I can
into them. Fall Tour answers one question: what's stopping me from visiting
five cities in the span of a few weeks and meeting a hundred new people?

This year is the biggest one yet. I've committed to staying in Las Vegas until
at least 2028, so I need ways of breaking up the year that remind me what
kind of world I'm working for. The tour is not a vacation. It's a campaign of
small objectives inside one story, and each leg below is one act of it.

## What I'm after

- Check in with people I already know, starting with five friends from UT
  Dallas and someone from a previous tour.
- Talk to fifty strangers and come home with three new friends.
- Make five memories with other people that outlast the trip, and one that
  justifies the whole thing.
- Bring back media for Las Vegans for Better Transit, for the Rebuilding
  America Project, and for The Willie Diaries.

## How it works

Every day has one primary objective and blocks of unstructured time so that
serendipity has room. Travel is mostly by train and bus, because that's how I
meet people, and because frugality is a good excuse for adventure.
