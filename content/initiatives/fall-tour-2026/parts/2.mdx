Somehow, two people I knew in very different parts of undergrad have both
ended up at Columbia. That feels like a pretty good reason to go to New York.

One was my mentee. The other worked with me in Nebula Labs and is now pursuing
an MBA. I'll get to see them in a city we didn't know each other in, which is
most of the appeal of this part.

## A fairly convincing excuse

Lawrence is playing three nights at Brooklyn Paramount, and I have a pass for
all three. Calling this a Staycation is doing a lot of work for someone flying
in from Las Vegas.

I'll get to spend Halloween in New York, too. Between the shows, I'll be
catching up with friends and getting to know people who already call the city
home.

## More reasons to come back

I want to make a new friend, or find a group of friends, in New York. The next
visit doesn't have to wait for another concert announcement.

The New York City Marathon will be my last stop before heading home on Sunday.
I'll be watching this one from the sidelines, with my own December races still
ahead of me. Being there won't require running 26.2 miles this time.
