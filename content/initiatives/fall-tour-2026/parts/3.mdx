Someone I care about is getting married. I'm looking forward to being there
for more than a quick congratulations.

Getting to Texas has somehow turned into the longest trip of the tour. There
are a few people to see first, and I wasn't going to pass up the trains on the
way home.

## One more show

Before the wedding, I'll be at Lawrence's Staycation finale at The Novo in Los
Angeles. After three nights in Brooklyn, stopping at the other end of the tour
feels entirely reasonable.

Chicago comes next for time with family. I want a few ordinary days together,
with more time to catch up than a phone call allows.

## Then we clean up a little

The wedding weekend is in Mansfield. I'll be back in North Texas for the
ceremony, ready to celebrate and find out what attending weddings as an adult
is supposed to look like.

## A very predictable detour

Afterward, I'll have a few days in the Bay Area. Caltrain, San Jose, Mount
Davidson, and Golden Gate Park are on the list. I'm bringing a camera for Las
Vegans for Better Transit and giving myself time to ride and walk.

Traveling all that way and ignoring the trains would be out of character.
The return to Las Vegas will be overland, too.
