The Willie Diaries is a lifelong story of one man's cautiously optimistic
journey to make the world a better place while living as meaningful of a life
as possible, told through the media platforms that define the era of the main
character.

That's the whole idea. The main character is me. The eras are whatever the
internet looks like at the time: a YouTube channel in college, Reels and
Stories now, and whatever comes next. The story doesn't end, so the diary
doesn't either.

## Where it's happening right now

Fall Tour 2026 is the current campaign. It's four acts across the country
this autumn, and every leg feeds the diary.
