A superbloom is what happens when seeds that have sat dormant in desert soil
for years all germinate at once. Project Superbloom is my version of that: a
season where the work I've been sitting on ships together.

The premise is simple. I'm going to launch four different software products
in a single month, not because the world needs four more apps, but because I
need to prove to myself that I can start doing big things again.

## The goals, a little facetiously

- Do more things.
- Do more important things.
- Do things more quickly.

The story of how it goes is a series of writings, starting with Part 1.
