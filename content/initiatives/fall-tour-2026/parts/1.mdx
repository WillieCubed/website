Dallas doesn't need much of a sales pitch for me. It's where I went to
college, and I still know enough people there to make a weekend feel too short.

Boys Go to Jupiter's final Now You're Just a Circle show gave me the excuse
for this visit. A night at Rubber Gloves in Denton and a weekend around friends
from UT Dallas were an easy plan to say yes to.

## Everyone gets a slide deck

I also put Dallas PowerPoint Night on the calendar. I wanted to get friends in the
same place and let everyone spend a little too long on something they care
about.

The presentations are only part of it. I'm interested in what makes a gathering
worth coming back to, including the invitations and the follow-up. I wanted to
try that with people I already had a reason to see.

## Of course there are trains

I also made plans to ride the Silver Line and compare airport access for Las
Vegans for Better Transit. Dallas has two airports and a growing rail network.
That was enough to add a transit detour to a concert weekend.

Between that and The Willie Diaries, there was always going to be a camera
involved.
