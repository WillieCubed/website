The first leg keeps it simple. I'm going to Dallas for a Boys Go to Jupiter
show, sleeping on a friend's couch, and spending the rest of the time with
the people from UT Dallas who get me a little more than most.

I don't have many intellectually curious friends in Las Vegas yet, so this
part of the tour is about maintaining the friendships that already exist and
getting out of my own head. Between hangouts I'll be riding DART with a camera
so that Las Vegans for Better Transit has a backlog of what a real light rail
network looks like.

## Outcomes I'm aiming for

- A produced episode of The Willie Diaries.
- Stronger friendships with the DFW crowd.
- A catalog of transit and urbanism footage from Dallas.
