I have a bad habit of turning concert tickets into much larger plans. A show
becomes a weekend, a weekend becomes an excuse to see someone, and suddenly
I'm spending the fall explaining why I'm headed out of town again.

The tickets make it easy to put something on the calendar. The harder part is
making time for the people I keep saying I'll visit. Fall Tour is my attempt
to do both.

## Go see your friends

Some of us met at UT Dallas. Some of us haven't lived in the same city for
years. I want more time together than a quick catch-up before everyone gets
busy again.

There's also room for people I haven't met. A new friend in New York would be
a pretty good reason to come back.

## And bring a camera

The Willie Diaries will come along. So will my interest in how cities work:
Dallas's airport connections and the Bay Area's trains deserve a closer look
for Las Vegans for Better Transit.

By the time I get home from Honolulu, I'll have run a half marathon and a full
marathon a week apart. Apparently the concerts weren't enough.
