I want my posts to live at URLs I control. I have added note publishing through
Micropub, an author card and entry markup that other sites can parse, and RSS,
Atom, and JSON feeds. The site also accepts Webmentions for moderation and sends
them when a post links to another site.

I tested the writing flow on an isolated deployment with its own database,
media store, and publishing branch. A Micropub request created a Git commit.
That commit deployed without a manual rebuild, and the new permalink appeared
in the feeds. A subscriber at WebSub Rocks received the RSS update after the
deployment. I also checked that removing a link sends a fresh Webmention to
the former target and that removing a link from a mention source removes the
approved interaction from the post.

Those tests show that the pieces can work together. I still need to publish
original posts on willie.page and link any copies on other services back to
their originals before I claim an IndieMark level for the public site.
